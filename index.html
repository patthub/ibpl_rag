iPBL RAG: example answers

Twenty-four questions about texts from Polish literary blogs and online magazines (the iPBL collection). For each question the system retrieved eight passages, and Gemma 4 E2B, running on a laptop, wrote the answer from them. Answers are printed as generated, without corrections.

The questions were asked in Polish and translated here. Every source can be opened to show the passage the model was given.
