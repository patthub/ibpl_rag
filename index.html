iPBL RAG — example questions and answers

The system answers questions about the Polish literary internet. For each question it retrieves the 8 most relevant article fragments (hybrid search, filters extracted from the question, a reranker), and a local language model, Gemma 4 E2B, writes an answer citing those sources. The search runs on the original Polish questions; the sources are in Polish.
